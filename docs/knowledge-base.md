## PlastiBytes
- Based in Buea, Cameroon (Mayor Street). Contact: info@plastibytes.com, +237 695 422 270.
- Recycles plastic bottles into high-quality 3D-printer filament, made locally and affordable.
- Mission: "Building Africa's sustainable future by transforming waste into innovation, one recycled bottle at a time."
- Services: plastic recycling, filament production, AI-powered waste sorting (robots with AI vision), support for hardware startups and maker communities.
- Impact: 125,000+ plastic bottles recycled, 4,000+ kg of waste diverted from landfills, 47+ hardware startups supported, 286+ jobs created.
- Team: Mbah Matt-Klaus (Founder & CEO), Musi Princess-Shanelle (Mentorship Manager), Asongna Brilan (Product Manager), Tambu Precious and Dzekamo Valentine (Product Designers), Tati Nelly (Community Manager), Nyanga Peniel A. (Head of Partnerships).
- Free guide: "How to Start a Hardware Startup in Cameroon" (on the website).

## PlastiBytes Mentorship (plastibytes.com/mentorship)
- "Become Africa's Next Hardware Innovator": 3D printing, robotics and product development, mentored by practitioners ("builders, not professors").
- 2,000+ graduates, 47+ startups launched, 6 African countries.
- Six modules: 1) Foundations of 3D Printing, 2) CAD & 3D Modeling (Fusion 360, FreeCAD), 3) Rapid Prototyping, 4) Robotics & Automation (Arduino, ESP32, sensors, IoT), 5) Product Development & Business, 6) Launch & Scale (pitching, grants such as Tony Elumelu, Mastercard Foundation).
- Plans: Community (free), Student FCFA 15,000/month (group calls twice a month, resources, certificate), Pro FCFA 45,000/month (1-on-1 sessions, WhatsApp access to a mentor, investor and grant introductions).
- No experience needed. English and/or French. Payment: MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, bank transfer, card.
- Success stories: Ngum Love (University of Buea, now at an IoT startup), Derick Fon (COLTECH Bamenda, FCFA 800,000 first-year revenue), Awa Njomo (IUGET Douala, Tony Elumelu grant).
- School and university group packages: email info@plastibytes.com, subject "Group Mentorship".

## Blog story: Ngum Love's water level monitor
- A computer engineering student built an automated water-level monitor for her final-year project: it switches the pump off before the tank overflows and on when it runs low.
- Built with Espressif (ESP32) tools and a 3D-printed enclosure made from PlastiBytes filament.
