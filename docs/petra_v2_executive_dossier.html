01 / PEDAGOGICAL REACH

"Think Big": Learning Across All Educational Tiers

Petra v2 is not a toy; it is an adaptive intellectual partner. Through dynamic prompt adjustment and interchangeable physical sensor kits, Petra scales its pedagogy from 8-year-old primary school discoverers to 24-year-old startup engineers prototyping market-ready products.

K-5

Primary School

Curiosity & Nature

Interactive "Show & Tell" using the ring-light camera. Petra identifies leaves, rocks, and recyclable plastics. Gamified multiple-choice quizzes on the color screen.

6-12

Secondary & High

Electronics & Coding

Ohm's law schematics, breadboard continuity testing, and basic robotics. Students write code and observe real-world execution on the wireless rover chassis.

Univ

Higher Education

Embedded IoT & CAD

ESP32 firmware architecture, Fusion 360 parametric design, 3D printing tolerances, and remote agricultural sensor telemetry networks.

Pro

Startup Incubator

Hardware Founders

Mentorship Module 6: "Launch & Scale." Product costing, grant writing (Tony Elumelu, Mastercard Foundation), low-volume manufacturing, and DFM validation.

02 / HANDS-ON CREATION

Real-World 3D-Printed Projects Built with Petra

Petra connects the digital AI tutor to physical creation. Students use PlastiBytes 100% recycled PET filament (made locally from discarded water bottles) to 3D print real functional machinery guided by Petra’s step-by-step display.

Robotics & Biomechanics

3D-Printed Bionic Robotic Gripper

Students print articulate finger joints from recycled PET, wire servo motors to Petra's MCP expansion bus, and program touch reflexes.

Clean Water & IoT

Automated Water Tank Controller

Inspired by Ngum Love's university project: a 3D-printed weatherproof enclosure housing ultrasonic water sensors to prevent village tank overflows.

Renewable Energy

Mini Wind Turbine & Solar Tracker

Aerodynamic 3D-printed turbine blades coupled with small DC dynamos. Petra monitors power output and teaches real-time energy conversion.

♻️ Closing the Loop on African Plastic Waste

Over 125,000 plastic bottles have already been collected and diverted from Cameroon’s gutters by PlastiBytes. Petra demonstrates circular economics live: students shred water bottles, extrude filament, and print their own lab equipment.
